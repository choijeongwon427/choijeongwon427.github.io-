## Brain MRI Harmonization

This project focuses on reducing domain variations in brain MRI images acquired from different imaging centers and scanners.

The primary objective is to improve MRI image consistency while preserving clinically important lesion information.

The research is based on the IGUANe deep learning framework, with additional strategies to enhance lesion preservation and image quality.

## Research Activities

- Investigated deep learning-based MRI harmonization techniques.
- Explored lesion-preserving fine-tuning methods using the IGUANe framework.
- Developed MRI preprocessing and evaluation pipelines.
- Evaluated image quality using PSNR, SSIM, and lesion preservation metrics.
- Conducted experiments using internal and external MRI datasets.
